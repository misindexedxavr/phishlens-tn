How scoring works

Every analysis runs a set of small, documented rules. Each rule that fires adds its weight to the score (or subtracts it, for mitigations). The total is clamped between 0 and 100 and mapped to a level.

    {% for name, minimum in levels %}
  • {{ name }} from {{ minimum }}
    • {% endfor %}

A clean result is not proof of safety, and a high score is not proof of fraud. Treat PhishLens as a second opinion and confirm through your bank or operator's official channel.

What PhishLens never does

  • It never executes JavaScript or renders the page.
  • It never submits a form or follows a form action.
  • It never stores credentials. Analysis results live only in your browser unless you export them.
  • It refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses, including after every redirect.
